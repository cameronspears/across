Across Send a file

Send files between a phone and a computer, no app

AirDrop only works between Apple devices, and Quick Share mostly between Android phones and Windows PCs. Across works in any current browser, so an iPhone can send to a Windows PC, or an Android phone to a Mac, with nothing to install.

From your phone to your computer

  1. On the computer, open acrossfile.com and leave it on the home page.
  2. On the phone, open acrossfile.com in Safari or Chrome, press Pick a file, and choose a photo, video or file.
  3. The phone shows a six-letter code, like PZK MFS. Type it into the box on the computer and press Receive.
  4. Press Download on the computer. Keep the phone’s screen on and the page open until it finishes.

Send a file now

From your computer to your phone

  1. On the computer, open acrossfile.com and drop the file on the page.
  2. Point the phone’s camera at the QR code on the screen and open the link. No camera app? Open acrossfile.com on the phone and type the six-letter code instead.
  3. Press Download on the phone. On an iPhone, a photo or video gets Save to Photos, so it lands in your library rather than in Files.

AirDrop and Quick Share are built into the system and work offline, which Across can’t match: it needs an internet connection to introduce the two devices. If both your devices support one of them, use it.

Good to know

  • On the same Wi-Fi, the file travels over your local network and never leaves it, so it is usually fast.
  • Keep the sending phone’s screen on. A locked phone pauses the page, and the transfer with it. Across asks the phone to stay awake while it sends.
  • Open links in a real browser like Safari or Chrome. The built-in browsers inside apps such as Instagram, Facebook or TikTok often drop downloads, and the page will say so.
  • One file at a time. For a whole album, zip it first, or send the photos one by one.
  • There is no account, so nothing links your phone and computer together afterwards. Each transfer is a fresh, encrypted connection.
